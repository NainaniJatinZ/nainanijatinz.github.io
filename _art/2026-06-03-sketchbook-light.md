Replicating art is fun, I wonder is it can be classified as creative, theres some minute decision making involved. but likely im learning more hand eye coordination than anything. 

It is also very calming! 

The idea of holding the page in front of the light came from noticing how thin the paper was. And the first page I was trying some simple shapes. So I think this image turned out to show more of a story / journey, however small it is.
