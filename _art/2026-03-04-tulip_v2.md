Reference: [https://drawbotanical.com/tulips/](https://drawbotanical.com/tulips/)

This was so much fun! Got to learn some cool botany facts while drawing. Kept the shading to the minimal and it definitely helped. 

However, still I am finding myself be too literal when copying from reference. Like, I am trying to figure out the exact angle and distance for the strokes to look exactly like the ref. I should lift those restrictions at some point.
